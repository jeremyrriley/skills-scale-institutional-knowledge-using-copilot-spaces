# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Technical Leads / Architects

### Role Summary
Technical Leads and Architects guide technical design and integration so the solution can meet product goals reliably and sustainably.

### Responsibilities
- Define and communicate the technical approach and architecture decisions
- Identify technical risks, integration points, and dependencies
- Support developers with design decisions and code quality
- Ensure the design is testable, operable, and aligned with agreed constraints

### Collaboration with Existing Roles
- Work with the Product Manager to translate outcomes and acceptance criteria into technical scope
- Coordinate with the Project Manager on estimates, dependencies, and technical risks
- Guide developers during implementation and partner with QA on testability and integration coverage
- Surface technical trade-offs and constraints to stakeholders through the Product Manager and Project Manager

---

## UX / Product Designers and Researchers

### Role Summary
UX and Product Designers and Researchers ensure that solutions address user needs and provide usable, accessible experiences.

### Responsibilities
- Plan or conduct user research and synthesize findings
- Create interaction designs, prototypes, and usability or accessibility guidance
- Validate design assumptions and incorporate user feedback
- Define experience-related acceptance criteria with the product team

### Collaboration with Existing Roles
- Partner with the Product Manager to connect user needs, product outcomes, and backlog priorities
- Engage stakeholders to gather domain knowledge and validate proposed experiences
- Work with developers to clarify designs and feasible implementation options
- Coordinate with QA to verify usability and accessibility expectations during acceptance testing

---

## Release / DevOps Engineers

### Role Summary
Release and DevOps Engineers support reliable delivery by maintaining deployment pipelines, environments, and operational readiness.

### Responsibilities
- Maintain or improve build, test, and deployment automation
- Prepare deployment environments and coordinate release readiness
- Support monitoring, post-deployment verification, and rollback procedures
- Identify operational risks and improve release reliability

### Collaboration with Existing Roles
- Coordinate with developers on build and deployment requirements and with QA on test environments and smoke tests
- Work with the Project Manager on release timing, dependencies, and readiness tracking
- Partner with the Product Manager on release scope and verification of intended outcomes
- Coordinate operational handoffs with stakeholders and support teams as appropriate

---

## Security / Privacy Leads

### Role Summary
Security and Privacy Leads help teams identify and address security, privacy, and compliance risks throughout delivery.

### Responsibilities
- Identify applicable security, privacy, and compliance requirements
- Advise on risk assessment, secure design, and appropriate controls
- Track security and privacy findings through mitigation and verification
- Recommend escalation when a risk requires specialist or leadership attention

### Collaboration with Existing Roles
- Partner with Technical Leads and developers on secure design and remediation
- Coordinate with QA on security-related verification and release checks
- Work with the Project Manager to record risks, owners, and escalations
- Advise the Product Manager on risk trade-offs and the impact of mitigation on scope or timing

---

## How these personas are used in the exercise
- Use these persona definitions to frame scenarios and sample interactions in the Skills Exercise.
- Each persona can be used as a persona prompt for Copilot Spaces to shape role-specific guidance.
- Teams may combine or delegate these roles based on project size, but should name an accountable owner for each responsibility.
